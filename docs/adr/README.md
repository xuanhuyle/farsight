# FarSight Architecture Decision Records

These thirty-one records elaborate the decisions committed in [`FARSIGHT_FOUNDATION_PLAN.md`](../../FARSIGHT_FOUNDATION_PLAN.md) into a form an implementer can build from. The plan says *what* FarSight is and why; these say *how*, and — more importantly — what each choice cost and what would make us reverse it.

**Every record is `Accepted 2026-08-28`.** The set was reviewed and accepted as a whole, because the records interlock: several decide questions the plan did not reach, and many of them request an amendment to the plan itself (see [Plan amendments](#plan-amendments-requested) below). Those amendments are approved but have not been written back into `FARSIGHT_FOUNDATION_PLAN.md`; where the two disagree, the ADR is the decision of record and the plan is the earlier draft.

Accepted does not mean immutable. Every record carries dated revisit triggers, and changing an accepted decision means a new record that supersedes it — never an edit to the Decision of one already accepted.

## How to read this set

Start with **ADR-013** for the shape of the codebase, then **ADR-001** and **ADR-007** for why the product exists at all — content-addressed identity and the evidence package are the two records the commercial thesis rests on. **ADR-004** is the scientific heart: the type system that makes epistemic uncertainty structurally impossible to launder into a probability.

By area: ADR-002, ADR-003 and ADR-018 for how engines are driven and composed; ADR-005, ADR-006, ADR-019 and ADR-022 for determinism, tiers, the reference container and sampling; ADR-008 and ADR-009 for numerics and the metric/threshold split; ADR-015, ADR-016, ADR-017 and ADR-020 for time, kernels, the naming authority and channels; ADR-010 and ADR-023 for faults and run outcomes; ADR-021 for comparison against real observations; ADR-011, ADR-012, ADR-014 and ADR-024 for storage, security posture, toolchain and the CLI; ADR-025 for the LLM exclusion. Then ADR-026 for model identity and validity envelopes, ADR-027 for declared scenario structure and group addressing, ADR-028 for the canonical observable vocabulary, ADR-029 for derived design inputs, and ADR-030 for how review works while no external domain experts are available.

**ADR-000** defines the template and the two non-standard sections that matter: a per-sub-decision confidence table with dated revisit triggers, and an Enforcement section naming the mechanical check and the week it first goes green. Both exist because this product's credibility rests on rules being *prevented*, not policed.

## The records

| # | Title | Status | What it decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| [000](ADR-000-adr-process-and-template.md) | ADR process and template | Accepted | Decisions are numbered Markdown records on a seven-section template; escape hatches from mechanical enforcement are two literal tokens, and human sign-offs land as `review_signoffs` rows inside hashed documents rather than living as norms. |
| [001](ADR-001-content-addressed-identity.md) | Content-addressed identity and the freeze protocol | Accepted | Identity is SHA-256 over RFC 8785 canonical JSON with **no JSON floats at all** in a hashed document, quantities as validated decimal strings plus unit, NaN and Infinity forbidden, timestamps and freezing identity outside the hash, and `(experiment_hash, run_index) → spec_hash` a pure derivation rather than a database join. |
| [002](ADR-002-process-isolated-workers.md) | Process-isolated workers as the universal execution unit | Accepted | Every RunSpec executes in one fresh OS process under `spawn` with `max_tasks_per_child=1`, thread and locale policy set before NumPy imports, crashes recorded as a ledger status rather than raised, and exactly one tested escape hatch (`reusable_worker`, SPICE only). |
| [003](ADR-003-engine-adapter-contract.md) | Two-level engine adapter contract, capability flags, refusal | Accepted | Adapters implement one minimal `Engine` protocol plus optional capability protocols with declared flags the planner reads; fault lowering has exactly four modes ending in refusal at freeze; there is no universal physics ontology and no engine-owned sampling. |
| [004](ADR-004-uncertainty-belief-model.md) | Belief tagged union, two-loop propagation, EpistemicCollapse | Accepted | Uncertainty is a five-member discriminated union with no `.to_distribution()` on epistemic kinds; propagation is a deterministic outer epistemic scan around a seeded inner aleatory Monte Carlo reporting an empirical p-box that carries its own convergence evidence; epistemic-to-probabilistic conversion exists only as a human-authorized, taint-propagating record. |
| [005](ADR-005-seeding-and-replay.md) | Seeding scheme and deterministic replay addressing | Accepted | One 128-bit root seed derives every stream through `SeedSequence` spawn keys of (run index, stream id) with Philox; exactly three streams; all randomness is pre-planned into RunSpecs and every derived value archived, so replay reads the archive rather than re-deriving. |
| [006](ADR-006-reproducibility-tiers.md) | Reproducibility tiers A/B/C and determinism rules | Accepted | Every claim is stamped Tier A (bitwise in the pinned environment), B (cross-platform tolerances at defined epochs) or C (cross-engine physical tolerances); no hashed value depends on completion order, clock, iteration order, PID or hostname; and a golden number may never originate from our own code, enforced by an attestation sidecar with four admissible sources. |
| [007](ADR-007-evidence-package-format.md) | Evidence package format, registers, claim statement | Accepted | A package is a directory of plain files under a one-level Merkle root carrying the exact falsifiable claim statement with a computable falsifier plus the mandatory registers, declaring its input closure, and verifiable — though not replayable — on a zero-extras install. |
| [008](ADR-008-units-and-numerics.md) | Units at boundaries, SI float64 core, numerical conventions | Accepted | Units are declarative metadata validated at schema boundaries and normalized once at construction; the numeric core is raw SI float64 with no unit library importable; each adapter owns a tested conversion table; reductions run in sorted order with compensated summation. |
| [009](ADR-009-metrics-and-acceptance-rules.md) | Metric and acceptance-rule separation, versioning, failure signatures | Accepted | A metric is a versioned pure function with **no thresholds**, identified by its declarative definition and pinned by AST hash; thresholds live only in separately versioned acceptance criteria; verdicts are three-valued with `indeterminate` a result; failure grouping is exact signature match, never clustering. |
| [010](ADR-010-fault-model.md) | FaultMode / FaultActivation / FaultActivationRecord, common cause | Accepted | Faults split three ways across the planes, every stochastic fault quantity is a `Belief` so no second uncertainty system exists, triggers use a closed predicate AST with no embedded Python, common cause is one layer of shared latent factors, and a missing engine binding fails at freeze. |
| [011](ADR-011-storage-and-persistence.md) | Storage: files, `.npy` channels, a SQLite ledger | Accepted | Three stores with separated roles; channels are NPY 1.0 `<f8` C-order hashed over a FarSight header plus payload rather than the container; every write is atomic with the ledger as sole crash arbiter; workers never write SQLite; migration is on-read and packages are never rewritten. |
| [012](ADR-012-offline-security-foundations.md) | Offline-first, no telemetry, signing seam, audit log, logging | Accepted | Runner and verifier make zero network calls and acquisition is a separate `fetch` path; no phone-home code path exists at all; the hash tree ships with a no-op signer attached outside it; every CLI mutation appends to a hash-chained audit log; logs are JSONL with locals-free tracebacks and are deliberately not package content. |
| [013](ADR-013-package-boundaries.md) | Modular monolith: package boundaries and dependency rules | Accepted | One `farsight` distribution in src layout with engines behind optional extras and import-linter contracts in CI from the first commit — including the commercially decisive rule that `evidence/` and `hashing/` never import `engines/`, so an auditor verifies a package on a clean install. |
| [014](ADR-014-toolchain-and-python-version.md) | Toolchain, Python version, dependency pinning, fixture corpus | Accepted | uv plus hatchling with one committed cross-platform `uv.lock` whose hash and bytes both ship in every package; Python pinned at 3.12, the ceiling of the GMAT/Basilisk intersection; and a three-class fixture-retention rule with a repo-size guard live from the first commit. |
| [015](ADR-015-time-and-frames.md) | Time scales, epoch representation, frames, aberration | Accepted | The canonical hashed epoch is TDB seconds past J2000 in one `Epoch` type, durations are a separate type, UTC is an input converted once at freeze into unhashed provenance, frames are explicit, and **aberration correction is a mandatory hashed field with no default anywhere in the code path**. |
| [016](ADR-016-kernel-sets.md) | Kernel sets: identity, furnish order, metakernels, coverage, cache | Accepted | The hashed run input is an **ordered** `KernelSet`, so reordering is an identity change and a set-hash can never claim reproducibility it does not have; metakernels are an import format and never a runtime artifact; coverage is attested and refused at freeze; the kernel cache is a fourth store. |
| [017](ADR-017-system-topology.md) | SystemTopology: node model, path grammar, parameter binding | Accepted | One frozen document of untyped named nodes with a constrained dot-separated path grammar; the byte-wise sort over paths fixes the aleatory draw order, which is materialized into the frozen design; and four mechanical rules keep it a namespace rather than a spacecraft ontology. |
| [018](ADR-018-run-composition.md) | Run composition: geometry provider and engine stages in one run | Accepted | A `RunSpec` carries an ordered stage list with at most one engine stage last, `engine_id` is derived rather than stored, stage inputs may name only earlier stages or hashed artifacts so the pure derivation survives, and geometry is materialized as ordinary channels an engine-free auditor can recompute from. |
| [019](ADR-019-reference-container.md) | The reference container and Tier-A environment identity | Accepted | One digest-pinned image; the Tier-A predicate is a **measured** `numeric_environment_hash` over mapped shared objects, BLAS and enabled ISA features rather than the image digest; runtime CPU dispatch is pinned to a baseline so a mixed CI fleet is fine and a below-baseline machine is refused by name. |
| [020](ADR-020-channel-model.md) | Channels: name grammar, sample grid, metadata | Accepted | A channel name is a topology path plus a leaf, stage-qualified, never carrying a unit suffix; one declared sample grid per experiment with no interpolation in the truth loop; and the grid digest enters the channel hash, closing a tamper-evidence hole where a channel on a different time base verified clean. |
| [021](ADR-021-referent-semantics.md) | Referent, ReferentPoint, and referent-comparison semantics | Accepted | A referent is an immutable transcription of a hash-pinned artifact with per-value locators, its stated uncertainty is a `Belief` rather than a tolerance number, alignment is declared with no model-side interpolation, and **every point declares whether it could have falsified anything**. |
| [022](ADR-022-distributions-and-sampling.md) | Distribution families, parameterization, sampler stream stability | Accepted | FarSight consumes only Philox raw words and implements every transformation itself — uniforms, inverse CDFs, Fisher-Yates, the copula Cholesky — with physical dimensioned parameters and no `loc`/`scale`, so a drawn value is a function of FarSight source and IEEE-754 rather than of a SciPy minor version. |
| [023](ADR-023-run-outcomes.md) | Run outcomes: taxonomy, resume, aggregation, error hierarchy | Accepted | Six ledger statuses with `failed`/`diverged` properties of the run and `crashed` a property of the machine; divergence declared in advance; resume re-executes everything but verified `ok`; and excluded runs enter the aggregate as **bounding mass** rather than being silently dropped, which would be survivorship bias inside the p-box. |
| [024](ADR-024-cli-surface.md) | CLI surface, exit-code contract, output modes | Accepted | Groups for durable object classes and bare verbs only where the target is unambiguous, each command mapping to exactly one audit action; an append-only exit-code registry distinguishing tamper from schema-invalid from missing-register; and verb names treated as a compatibility surface, because they are printed into shipped packages. |
| [025](ADR-025-no-llm-sdk.md) | No LLM SDK: the exclusion as a structural absence | Accepted | No LLM SDK is a dependency of this repository in any extra including `dev`, so "is an LLM installed?" is answerable `no` from a lock file with no qualifier; the never-list is written now while nobody wants anything from it; and the claim we may make is about the lock file, never about behaviour. |
| [026](ADR-026-model-and-engine-build.md) | Model, ModelVersion, EngineBuild, and the validity envelope | Accepted | Three content-addressed knowledge-plane objects giving `ModelVersionRef` a referent before the first freeze, and giving the corpus's central principle — physics belongs to models — an object to hang on: a validity envelope with mechanical ranges and prose conditions, whose excursions are flagged and registered but never blocking. |
| [027](ADR-027-scenario-enumerations.md) | Declared scenario enumerations, group addressing, factor-coupled parameters | Accepted | A scenario may declare named enumerations carrying only names, epochs, windows and node paths; they give `sampling_scope` its missing referent, let one authored object expand at freeze into N materialized activations or bindings, anchor triggers to a member's epoch, and let a shared latent factor modulate an ordinary parameter. |
| [028](ADR-028-observable-vocabulary.md) | Canonical observable vocabulary | Accepted | The founder's minimum vocabulary as *data* — content-addressed `ObservableDef`s carrying prose conventions and a unit dimension, with no numeric fields ever — plus one optional `conforms_to` on a channel, consumed in the comparison layer where a mismatch is declared rather than refused. Reserves no name, so ADR-020's rejection of namespaces stands. |
| [029](ADR-029-derived-bindings.md) | Derived bindings and the arithmetic expression AST | Accepted | A design input that is a consequence of other inputs is declared as a derivation over a closed seven-node arithmetic AST, evaluated once at freeze over deterministic inputs and materialized with its expression; stating a quantity both directly and by derivation is refused, closing a silent self-contradiction that syntactic identity otherwise permits. |
| [030](ADR-030-deep-review-substitution.md) | Deep Review substitution under expert unavailability | Accepted | With no external domain experts available this phase, expert-review steps are replaced by a stored Deep Review protocol under the rule that missing expert review widens uncertainty, never narrows it; claims use a closed vocabulary (`research-reviewed`, never `expert validated`) enforced by lint, external review converts to deferred gates, and `EXPERT_REVIEW_BACKLOG.md` keeps the debt visible. |

## Plan amendments requested

The set carries `PLAN AMENDMENT REQUESTED:` lines resolving to roughly **forty-five unique amendments** against the foundation plan (thirty-eight from the original set, plus those introduced by ADR-026 through ADR-029). They are not applied — the plan is unmodified — and they want a single review pass rather than piecemeal approval. Two are worth deciding first because they unblock the most files:

- **Extras become `spice`, `basilisk`, `gmat`, `analysis`, `dev`** (plan §6), moving `spiceypy` out of the base install. Its wheel bundles a compiled CSPICE, so leaving it core falsifies the zero-engine auditor claim that three separate records call the most commercially important property in the codebase. Requested by nine records.
- **`ExperimentDesign` and `EvidencePackage` gain `review_signoffs`** (plan §4). Forty-two non-mechanizable rules across the set are otherwise norms with no home, and several of them gate `evidence_grade`. Requested by six records.

One amendment contradicts an already-written record rather than merely extending the plan, so it needs an explicit yes or no: **a fifth register, `registers/excluded_runs.json`** (plan §13). The existing four record what was assumed, unknown, judged and extrapolated; none records what was *not counted*, which is what a reader needs before trusting any distribution in the package.

## The architecture evolution review

ADR-026 through ADR-029 were commissioned by [`FARSIGHT_ARCHITECTURE_EVOLUTION_REVIEW.md`](../../FARSIGHT_ARCHITECTURE_EVOLUTION_REVIEW.md), which stress-tested this corpus against a system-of-systems reference architecture (a relay corridor of a thousand swarms launched over decades) and against a generality ladder running from one lunar spacecraft up to it. The review's finding was that the corpus needed four new records and about a dozen one-paragraph seams, and that its hardest-looking problems dissolve into the principle that physics belongs to models — populations are models, existence over time is state, connectivity is model output, generations are names plus latent factors.

That review is also where the standing **rejections** are recorded, and they are load-bearing: no graph-shaped topology, no `Population` or `Generation` entity, no network domain in the shared schema, no causal-graph objects, no optimizer plumbing, no per-entity schema objects. Several of them are now written into the records themselves — ADR-017 rule 8 is the main home — so that a future proposal has text to argue against rather than an accident to exploit.

## Adding or changing a record

Never edit an accepted record's Context, Decision, Options, Consequences or Enforcement sections. Write a new numbered record carrying `Supersedes ADR-NNN` and mark the old one `Superseded by ADR-NNN`. While the set is `Proposed`, records may still be edited in place — that is what this review is for. Every new record needs its row in this table in the same commit; `adr-lint` fails on a file without a row or a row without a file.
