# FarSight — Architecture Evolution Review

**Status:** For founder review. No ADR amendments applied; no code exists yet — the reviewed artifact is the 26-record Proposed ADR corpus.
**Date:** 2026-08-28
**Inputs:** FARSIGHT_FOUNDATION_PLAN.md; FARSIGHT_INTERSTELLAR_REFERENCE_ARCHITECTURE_v0.1.md; FARSIGHT_FOUNDER_FEEDBACK.md; docs/adr/ADR-000..025.

## 1. Executive conclusion

**The architecture survives the stress test, and the honest headline is that the reference architecture validates the Proposed corpus far more than it indicts it.** One fact frames everything: there is no code. The "current implementation" is the schema set frozen in the 26 Proposed ADRs — which is written to be directly implementable and is therefore genuinely reviewable — and every change this review recommends is a text amendment to a record awaiting founder review anyway. This is the cheapest moment this review will ever be possible: the same findings at month six would be identity-scheme changes to persisted evidence, which ADR-001/§16 make effectively permanent.

The verdict in one paragraph: the corpus contains remarkably little hard-coded N=1 — the entity concept is *absent* from the schemas rather than fixed at one, and its hardest-looking scale problems (a million probes, evolving relay graphs, dormancy, generations) dissolve into the already-frozen principle that **physics belongs to models**: populations are models, existence-over-time is state, connectivity is model output, generations are names plus latent factors. What the review found instead is **three RED items, all enumeration- or reference-shaped, none philosophical**: (R1) the `Model`/`ModelVersion`/`EngineBuild` schemas do not exist while `ModelVersionRef` already sits inside a hashed schema; (R2) `sampling_scope: "per_pass"` is a closed enum with a dangling referent — no record defines how a scenario declares a pass — and it bakes a comms-mission noun into the bottom-of-stack schema; (R3) fault/belief targeting of N sibling components is N hand-authored objects, and ADR-017's own confidence table says the expansion mechanism "must be decided before the first fault campaign is hashed" — which is week 5–6 of the MVP, so "later" is measured in weeks. All three are amendable today for the cost of a text edit; all three are hash-identity invalidations in six months.

Recommended: **four new ADRs (026–029), roughly a dozen one-paragraph seam amendments to existing Proposed records, zero implementation-scope change to the 8-week MVP, and an explicit rejection list** to defend against over-generalization — because the gravest risk surfaced by this review is not a missing abstraction but reviewers installing fleet-shaped ontology, optimizer plumbing, or scale infrastructure that the lunar spacecraft would then carry forever.

## 2. Architecture evolution acknowledged

FarSight was originally designed to be a verification and mission-evidence platform for autonomous deep-space spacecraft: orchestrate existing engines, produce reproducible seeded campaigns, separate aleatory from epistemic uncertainty structurally, inject faults with common-cause structure, and package independently verifiable evidence. The MVP embodiment is deliberately narrow — DSN/DSOC link benchmarks, a native link-chain engine, a minimal Basilisk adapter.

The reference architecture adds a categorically harder long-term referent: a system-of-systems (Earth infrastructure, accelerator, ~1,000 relay swarms launched over decades, a science swarm), populations rather than vehicles, heterogeneous hardware generations that must interoperate, a time-evolving communications network with regenerative relaying, dormancy/burst operations across 25+ year timelines, relativistic kinematics, high attrition with mandatory common-mode structure, spatially correlated environments, derived-not-duplicated design parameters, and multi-objective (Pareto) evaluation. Per the founder's framing — and this review holds it — the corridor is **not a product target**; it is the extreme stress test: if the foundations can represent it honestly, a 100-satellite autonomous swarm is easy.

The founder feedback simultaneously reframes the project as scientific-engineering-first (commercial validation informs the business model, not the project's continuation) and directs one architectural revision: a deliberately small canonical observable vocabulary. Both are reflected here (§14 of this review and §19/§25).

## 3. What remains unchanged

Every load-bearing principle survives contact with the corridor, and several turn out to be exactly what the corridor needs:

- **Physics belongs to engines/models.** This single principle dissolves most of the apparent stress: population dynamics, relay routing, membrane degradation, dust fields, relativistic clock behavior are all engine models behind ADR-003's opaque `config_ref`.
- **Evidence independent of engines** (auditor verifies on a zero-extras install) — unweakened; the corridor's evidence is channels, records, registers like any mission's.
- **Unknown is a valid state; no epistemic→probability laundering** — the reference architecture's §10/§17 demands are ADR-004's existing core, nearly verbatim.
- **Reproducibility and content-addressed identity** — untouched; notably, two deliberate design moves (ADR-011's container-independent channel hash, ADR-001's identity-excluded provenance block) turn out to be precisely the seams a corridor-scale future needs.
- **Refusal over silent approximation** — extended by this review, not weakened (freeze-time refusal of inconsistent derived parameters, of unresolvable groups).
- **No universal spacecraft ontology** — reaffirmed, with the founder's revised fence: *no ontology; only the minimum canonical vocabulary required for evidence and cross-engine comparison* (§14).
- **The 8-week MVP plan** — unchanged. Nothing in this review adds implementation scope before week 8.

## 4. New stresses introduced by Reference Architecture v0.1

Genuine architectural stressors (the complete list — everything else in the document is a modelling exercise the corpus already handles):

1. **Multiplicity of authored objects** — faults, beliefs, couplings, and nodes over N sibling components at N = 100–1,000 granularity (probes stay engine-internal; swarms/hops do not).
2. **Declared mission structure** — passes, hops, wake windows, deployment waves: recurring scenario partitions that sampling scopes, trigger anchors, and group targets all need to reference, and that no schema currently declares.
3. **Latent factors reaching parameters** — ADR-004 asserts shared-latent-factor dependence for *parameters*, but the only coupling schema in the corpus (ADR-010's `Coupling`) reaches fault activations only.
4. **Model identity as a first-class referent** — generations, model families, and fidelity levels all resolve to `ModelVersion` + validity envelope, which exists only as plan prose while `ModelVersionRef` already appears inside a hashed schema.
5. **Cross-fidelity consistency** — validating an aggregate population law against explicit-agent sample runs is model-vs-model comparison with no home (and one tempting, forbidden shortcut: laundering model output through `Referent`).
6. **Derived design inputs** — `launch_interval = relay_spacing / cruise_speed` must be computed, never duplicated; the corpus has no derived-input concept, and ADR-001's syntactic identity makes duplication undetectable by design.
7. **Observable semantics across engines** — the founder-directed vocabulary (§14).

Explicitly *not* stressors (dissolved on inspection): million-entity representation (§17), evolving network topology (§13, §14), relativistic time (§13), dormancy/burst timelines (ADR-020's `explicit` grid is the designed valve), Pareto optimization (§18), evidence causal chains (§16).

## 7. Fundamental changes recommended now (CHANGE NOW)

All are amendments to Proposed records or new Proposed records — zero implementation-scope change to the MVP.

**C1 — ADR-026: `Model` / `ModelVersion` / `EngineBuild` schemas (new record; the unambiguous one).**
`ModelVersionRef` already appears inside a hashed schema (`EpistemicSet.members: list[Quantity] | list[ModelVersionRef]`, ADR-004); ADR-014 prose relies on `EngineBuild`; the ModelVersion **validity envelope** — the single mechanism on which population-model honesty, generation model-families, and fidelity applicability all rest — exists only as plan-§4 prose. Freezing week-3 designs containing refs to an undefined object type is exactly the identity hazard ADR-021 fixed for `Referent`. Contents: content-addressed identity; `validity: ValidityEnvelope` (reusing ADR-004's type); engine binding (`config_dialect` targeted); verification status; the rule that a run exiting the envelope sets validity flags and lands in the violation register (wiring to ADR-023). *Retrofit at +6 months: identity-permanent (frozen designs + shipped packages fix the reference form). Ladder: invisible below Stage 3.*

**C2 — ADR-027: Declared scenario enumerations and group addressing (new record; one concept discharging findings 1, 2, 6 of §5).**
`ScenarioTemplate` gains declared, named enumerations — run-protocol data only: names plus epochs/members, no physical quantities, so the §2 ontology tripwire is not touched. Three consumers:
- `sampling_scope` becomes `"per_run" | "per_experiment" | {"per_group": "<name>"}` — giving `per_pass` its missing referent (a pass list is one such group), removing the comms-mission noun from bottom-of-stack `belief.py`, and generalizing to hops, wake windows, deployment waves. This is the same class of change the corpus refuses to defer for `Epoch.scale`: a closed enum in every belief-bearing document.
- Trigger anchors: `AfterElapsed.of` (ADR-010) admits a declared enumeration member, so "10 years after *this* wave's launch" is expressible; `Duration.anchor: Ref | None` already anticipates exactly this.
- **Group targeting with freeze-time expansion**: an optional design-plane block on `FaultCampaign`/`UncertaintySpec` whose only selector is subtree membership (`is_under` over customer-named paths — a generation *is* a subtree because generations are names the author chose), **materialized at freeze into fully enumerated activations/bindings recorded verbatim** (the `draw_order` pattern, third use), with a freeze validator recomputing the expansion. Frozen documents stay fully explicit: draw counts, `draw_order`, RunSpec pinning, and ADR-005 addressing are untouched; nothing wildcard-shaped ever enters a hashed document. Plus one optional `FaultActivation.group_id`, so ADR-009 failure signatures can see "these 1,000 activations are one batch defect" instead of degenerating into singleton groups. ADR-017's own confidence table says this must be decided "before the first fault campaign is hashed" — week 5–6. *Retrofit: schema_version bump under every design + fault catalog later; text today. Ladder: lunar case authors `per_run` and single targets exactly as now, zero burden.*
- Two schema-shape fixes ride in the same batch because they touch the same records and are free now / hash-bumps later: `Coupling`'s target retyped to a discriminated union (`{"activation": id} | {"factor": id}`) with one-layer enforced as a *freeze validator* rather than a schema impossibility (multi-layer semantics stay DEFERRED to ADR-010's own week-5 trigger); and **factor-coupled hyperparameters** — a fourth form in `Distribution.params` / belief hyperparameters, `{factor, law: multiplicative|additive|replace, coefficient: Belief}`, reusing ADR-010's exact vocabulary — closing the gap where ADR-004 *asserts* shared-latent dependence for parameters but no schema carries it (dust density across a corridor region; even two Stage-2 parameters sharing one epistemic coordinate).

**C3 — ADR-028: Canonical observable vocabulary (new record; founder-directed).**
Reconciles founder feedback §10 with ADR-020 Option 3's correct rejection of namespaces: **vocabulary as data, conformance as declaration, names stay customer-owned.** A knowledge-plane `ObservableDef` (content-addressed; `observable_id`, version, unit dimension, frame/timescale/reference-point convention statement, definition text; **no numeric fields, no defaults, no per-observable attribute schemas** — that is the ontology line, stated in the record) plus one optional field `ChannelDecl.conforms_to: Ref | None = None`. Enforcement lives in the comparison layer (a cross-engine `ComparisonSpec` may require matching `conforms_to`; mismatches land in declared-unmatched-items, never refusal), with an OBS-1 sign-off for the non-mechanizable residue. Registry contents (~15 entries seeded from the founder's list, with an ADR-010-style ceiling: "a further entry is a decision, not a feature") are DEFERRED to Stage 3 when GMAT arrives. *Retrofit: `ChannelDecl` is hashed inside `SystemTopology`, under every design hash — the field is free now and a knowledge-plane re-identification later. This is the clearest free-now/expensive-later case in the review.*

**C4 — Two one-line typing fixes in ADR-010:** `FaultActivationRecord.factor_state` keys fixed as factor ids resolved from the frozen campaign (the join for evidence chain-assembly is currently inferred, not defined); and `ChannelDecl` gains optional `component_labels: list[str] | None` (validated `len == components`) so the trailing axis — the corpus's strongest multi-entity seam — stops being anonymous: an auditor holding `corridor.hop_link_margin.npy` can say which hop column 42 is, and a fault record naming `swarm_0042` joins to a column. *Both free now; execution-plane/knowledge-plane bumps later.*

## 8. Extension seams to preserve now (ADD A SEAM NOW — one paragraph each, in Proposed records)

1. **Edges never on `TopologyNode`** (ADR-017): relationship edges, if ever introduced, are a separate content-addressed document referencing nodes by path; never node fields, never affecting path sort order or `draw_order`. Insurance against the month-4 `edges` field that would re-hash the knowledge plane and move every drawn value.
2. **Population-is-a-model** (ADR-017/003): a node may denote an ensemble; FarSight cannot and must not know the difference; aggregate population dynamics are engine physics behind `config_ref`; population terms (`count`, `attrition`, `survival`) join the no-physics-in-shared-schema denylist as field names.
3. **Lifecycle-channel convention** (ADR-020/023): entity birth/dormancy/retirement is a `code_map` status channel produced by the model — never node validity fields, and **never NaN** (absence is a code; non-finite still means `diverged`). Pre-empts both the `valid_from` node field and the attrition-poisons-the-aggregate failure.
4. **Cross-fidelity comparison** (ADR-021/006): a model-vs-model comparison (cross-engine, cross-fidelity, cross-vintage) is a future `ComparisonSpec` variant whose two sides are run sets named by `experiment_hash` + selector, reusing matched-config declaration, `grid_hash` identity, declared reductions, three-valued verdicts — and it is **never expressed by binding FarSight output as a `Referent`** (the shortcut a schedule-pressed Stage-3 implementer will reach for; after one shipped package does it, "referent numbers never originate from our code" is retroactively compromised).
5. **Derived bindings — ADR-029, defined now, implemented post-MVP:** `DerivedBinding{path, expression}` alongside `ParameterBinding`; expression is a closed five-operator arithmetic AST (leaves `{param: path}` | `{const: Quantity}`; `add|sub|mul|div|neg`; sibling of `PredicateAST`, same ceiling discipline — **not** an extension of it, which ADR-010 forbids twice, and not a CAS); evaluated once at freeze over `Deterministic` inputs, dimensional-checked, materialized as a `Deterministic` belief with `pedigree.level: "derived_analysis"` and the hashed expression as derivation record; **overspecification refused at freeze** (a path with both binding kinds fails, naming the path). This is the reference architecture's `launch_interval = spacing / speed` discipline made structural; today, duplication is undetectable by construction (`"0.220" != "0.22"`). Epistemic inputs (per-outer-point evaluation) named as growth path, built never-yet.
6. **Per-entity randomness stream** (ADR-005): one sentence naming per-entity realization in FarSight-native engines as the intended tenant of a reserved stream id (3–15); appending a stream moves nothing in streams 0–2 by the direct-addressing construction.
7. **Engine-side correlated environments** (ADR-022/010): correlation groups are for small parameter sets; a high-dimensional correlated field (dust along the corridor) is a model, seeded via `engine_module_seeds`, its correlation structure a pedigreed model input; a spatial profile over declared targets is K scalar factors with per-coupling coefficients — no field/mesh type, ever.
8. **Related packages by root hash** (ADR-007): a claim statement may cite other packages' root hashes; a cross-campaign claim is itself a package whose inputs are other packages' roots (the ADR-011 supersession idiom generalized). The future study-series link lives in the **unhashed provenance block** — identity-neutral forever; a hashed series field would make a design's identity depend on its search history and must never be built.
9. **Sanctioned optimization shape** (ADR-005 References): an external driver above the campaign layer emitting frozen `ExperimentDesign`s, each campaign one function evaluation, iterating in the `exploratory` lane with frontier points re-frozen attended into evidence-grade. Zero schema change, buildable any time.
10. **Zero-magnitude paired counterfactual** documented as *the* deterministic causal-attribution pattern (ADR-010): same design, same seeds, effect nulled — draws identical (AT-10 already proves byte-transparency), delta attributable to exactly one activation via `evidence diff`. Naive fault-on/off campaigns are not draw-matched (removing an activation shifts downstream draws); this pattern is.
11. **Negative naming rules** recording the §10 rejections (no `Population`/`Generation`/network entities in `farsight.schemas`), so PHYS-1/TOPO-1 reviewers have text to cite.

## 10. Changes explicitly rejected as overfitting

Each fails the generality ladder at L1 or breaches a standing tripwire: graph-shaped `SystemTopology` (destroys the total path order → draw order → every hashed drawn value; the corridor is topologically a *chain*); `Population` or `Generation` entities (universal-ontology tripwire in a fleet uniform; the lunar spacecraft becomes a population of cardinality 1); FarSight-owned edge vocabulary (`communicates_with`, `depends_on` — `shares_common_cause` already *is* `CommonCauseFactor`, and a duplicate edge mechanism nothing reconciles); a network/communications schema domain (routing is engine physics; the graph input is engine-native `config_ref`); stochastic-field machinery in core; causal-graph objects (§19's deliverable is the computed sensitivity artifact, not a declared DAG); canonical vocabulary as channel namespaces or shipped node names; wildcards/pattern-valued anything in hashed documents; per-probe schema entities; `PredicateAST` arithmetic; symbolic algebra/CAS dependencies; aleatory-over-aleatory hierarchies (explicit decomposition is *more* honest); proper-time `Epoch` scales (a relativistic clock is a modeled channel; the hashed timeline stays TDB — at 0.2c, γ ≈ 1.02, and the ref-arch's own §9 lists `clock_error` as a model input); interpolation/multi-rate grids in the truth loop; a `fidelity_level` capability flag; `draw_index` spawn-key extension; optimizer imports, adaptive sampling, campaign top-up; utility/weights fields on designs or criteria (preference scalarization inside a hashed scientific record is the single-number laundering ADR-004 exists to prevent); optimizer-tunable acceptance thresholds (automated tolerance-shopping — collides with the zero-count dishonesty kill); result-push APIs (offline-first); distributed execution infrastructure; delta/streaming package layouts; raising ADR-017's path caps preemptively (they are Windows-filename budgets with stated arithmetic).

## 11. SystemTopology / relationship-graph recommendation

**Keep the pure containment tree. Do not build a graph or a RelationshipGraph now. Reserve the seam in one paragraph.**

The tree survives all five ladder rungs *as a namespace*, and ADR-017's graph rejection is load-bearing, not aesthetic: a graph gives a node multiple paths, and the byte-wise path sort fixes the aleatory draw order materialized into `draw_order` — N paths per node makes drawn values depend on traversal, the exact dependency §12 outlaws, sitting directly under K3. The decisive reframing: the corridor's *communications graph over decades is not topology in FarSight's sense at all* — it is time-varying physical state (which relays are alive, in view, in acquisition), and under physics-belongs-to-models it lives inside the corridor model and comes out as channels. A static edge list in a hashed design could not represent an evolving relay graph anyway; only a model can.

Of the founder's candidate edge types: `shares_common_cause` already exists as `CommonCauseFactor` + `Coupling` (a topology edge would be a second common-cause mechanism nothing reconciles); `member_of_generation` is subtree naming today (ADR-026 supplies the model-identity half); `communicates_with`/`depends_on` are connectivity FarSight deliberately forecloses acting on — an edge FarSight stores but never dispatches on is a string we hash and print, near-zero value, nonzero ontology surface. Links are nodes (`formation.crosslink`, `corridor.hop_0042`) whose parameters name endpoints as opaque strings — ADR-017 Option 3's own idiom, promoted to Decision text.

What must be written now (the seam): *relationship edges, if ever introduced, are a separate content-addressed document referencing topology nodes by path; never fields on `TopologyNode`; never affecting path sort order or `draw_order`; hashed design-plane if anything numeric flows through them, context-plane if annotation-only.* The existing path-reference rule plus that sentence is the entire minimum abstraction. A future graph document is then constructible with zero change to `TopologyNode` and no re-hash of anything existing.

## 12. Entity / swarm / population recommendation

**No `Entity`/`Population` distinction in the schemas — now or, absent new evidence, later.** The key insight tested and confirmed against every schema that could force per-entity explicitness: **a population is a model.** A node `corridor.relay_population` (kind `physical` — legitimately fault-targetable) with `ParameterDecl`s (`n_probes_per_swarm`, `attrition_shape`, `common_mode_strength`) and `ChannelDecl`s (`surviving_fraction`, with `components: 1000` for the per-swarm axis) is well-formed *today*; the aggregate dynamics are an engine stage behind an opaque `config_ref`; its parameters are `Belief`s; model-family uncertainty ("independent-given-factors vs. clustered attrition") is `EpistemicSet.members: list[ModelVersionRef]` — the outer scan enumerates population-model families exactly as it enumerates any model family. Per-probe stochastic attrition is aleatory physics inside the model — the correct side of ADR-010's fault-vs-environment boundary — which also dissolves the state-dependent-hazard foreclosure for the corridor (velocity-dependent dust attrition is continuous engine physics, not a `StochasticHazard`).

**Hybrid mode** (100 explicit representatives + statistical remainder) is ordinary authored nodes plus one population node on one grid — the authoring tax is ADR-027's freeze-time expansion. **What keeps Population from becoming pseudo-physics in core:** ADR-017 §7's four mechanical rules plus the denylist extension (§8.2) — the moment FarSight core defines what a population *is* beyond "a node whose model happens to be aggregate," it owns population dynamics for every customer forever. No third `NodeKind`: `kind`'s only consumer is fault-target legality, and a population node is legitimately targetable, so `physical` is semantically correct.

Where explicit representation is ever genuinely needed — validating the aggregate law itself against an agent-based engine run of one swarm (agents engine-internal, still one run) — is cross-model comparison, which the corpus already owns (Tier C, matched-config, "aggregation itself" as the headline declared-unmatched item); it needs §8.4's seam, not an entity system. Per-probe channels are refused at freeze by the declared-channels discipline — a 10⁶-wide components axis is a design error the schema already catches.

## 13. Time/deployment model recommendation

**The time model is the corpus's strongest area — no schema change.** `Epoch` is absolute TDB (no shared-start assumption anywhere; float64 exact to ~0.1 µs at 25-year spans), so staggered launches over decades, asymmetric mission ages, and birth mid-mission are expressible today. The three findings:

1. **Existence-over-time is state, not structure**: "probe 7 does not exist before 2043" is physics, i.e. a lifecycle `code_map` channel produced by the model, usable in fault predicates and metric domain gates; birth/retirement events are records or channel transitions. Node validity fields (`valid_from`) would make the naming authority adjudicate world-state — the first value-bearing node field. Convention paragraph per §8.3.
2. **Per-entity mission age** ("degradation starts 10 years after *this* wave's launch") currently requires hand-computed absolute epochs or predicate workarounds — fixed by ADR-027's declared-enumeration anchors.
3. **Dormancy/burst over decades**: the `explicit` sample grid (`epochs_artifact`) is the designed valve — dense around relay bursts, sparse across dormancy; the grid samples *reporting* epochs, not physics steps. Channel volume (a 30-year fine-cadence uniform grid would be ~126 MB/channel/run) is a campaign-design concern instrumented by ADR-011's existing measurement rows, not a schema defect. Keep no-interpolation absolutely.

Relativistic time: rejected as a platform concern (§10) — one coordinate timescale, clock divergence as a modeled channel with a `Belief` error budget.

## 14. Communications-network boundary

**A network simulator is just another engine — and nothing in the corpus prevents it. That is the finding.** Verified: the `Engine` protocol fits a discrete-event network simulator exactly (`supports_stepping`, `run_to` advances the event queue, `collect()` emits grid-sampled channels); the routing table / contact plan / adjacency input is the engine's own `config_dialect` document — hashed, validated, never read by FarSight; the evolving graph is engine-computed state (`corridor.hop_0042.active` as a 0/1 channel); link-scoped observables are channels on link-as-node entities; end-to-end throughput, latency, availability, bottleneck-hop position (ref-arch §15 items 8–19) are metrics over channels, several being argmin/min reductions over a `components` axis. The one-engine-per-run rule does not block a link+network composition: a FarSight-native network engine internalizes the link chain as a library (engines own their physics; the link chain is pure Python and FarSight-owned), consuming geometry channels through `ChannelSource` bindings like the flagship. Lifting the engine cap remains ADR-018's own anticipated superseding record.

**Boundary statement for the record:** FarSight never owns route semantics, never answers "what is downstream of this node," and the network partition in the target evidence artifact is a model-emitted availability/connectivity channel with an `AcceptanceCriterion` over it — which is also where ADR-028's vocabulary (availability, backlog, node operational state) earns its place.

## 15. Common-cause / correlated uncertainty assessment

The latent-factor architecture is fundamentally right and the reference architecture's demands land on it cleanly — with two genuine gaps (both in the ADR-027 batch):

- **fault→swarm/generation/region/time-window**: group-scoped couplings via freeze-time expansion over customer-named subtrees (a generation is a subtree; a spatial region is a subtree the author structured; a time window is activation-splitting). Honest deflation: batch defects modeled at swarm/generation granularity are ~10³ activations, not 10⁶ — probes are engine-internal.
- **factor→parameter coupling**: asserted in ADR-004's prose, absent from schema — the fourth hyperparameter form closes it.
- **factor→factor (one-layer limit)**: partly deflated — "accelerator mis-pointing → swarm dispersion → relay gap" is *not* a factor chain; the chain runs through engine physics and `on_condition` triggers over engine channels. The genuine two-layer cases (temporal regimes; hierarchical defect structure) are ADR-010's own 0.60-confidence row with a week-5 trigger; the coupling-union shape change makes the eventual lift a validator relaxation instead of a fault-catalog re-identification.
- **Spatial/temporal/batch/hierarchical correlation**: spatial fields are K scalar factors + per-coupling coefficients (a discretized Karhunen–Loève expansion — expressible today, post-C2, with zero new types); temporal correlation rides the deferred two-layer semantics; batch correlation is `per_experiment`-scoped components combined in-engine plus `epistemic_existence` factors ("in both or neither — enumerated, never weighted", designed for exactly this); aleatory-over-aleatory stays rejected — explicit decomposition is more honest.

**Independent-Bernoulli collapse is structurally prevented** — the reference architecture's mandatory requirement was already ADR-010's founding constraint.

## 16. Evidence/provenance scalability assessment

**The causal chain the founder wants is already derivable — REJECT any causal-AI or `CausalChain` schema.** "Relay 417 lost aperture because gen-3 membrane degradation and correlated dust and a batch defect → partition at year 17.4" assembles by pure joins over mandated package content: factor realizations (`factor_state` + pre-drawn RunSpec values + `epistemic_bin` outer coordinates — "gen-3" is an outer coordinate captured verbatim); what fired and when (`FaultActivationRecord` with `trigger_evidence`); total event ordering by construction (one shared grid, one index axis — "year 17.4" is `epoch0 + i·step`); metric violations (`DivergenceReport.first_index`, failure signatures, excluded-runs register); every edge a content ref resolving inside the package. Missing pieces are a *renderer* (deferred, identity-neutral forever) and the §8.10 counterfactual pattern documentation. What signatures prove is co-occurrence; the deterministic "because" is the zero-magnitude paired counterfactual — an arithmetic claim, not an inference.

Register ergonomics at 1,000-swarm scale: registers scale with *declared* objects, not entities; at honest generation-granularity modeling the unknowns register is ~10² entries — readable; the 40,000-entry version is an authoring anti-pattern the corpus already resists. Grouping is a renderer concern.

## 17. Scaling implications

**The million never appears as a million of anything inside FarSight** — not runs, not nodes, not channels, not files. What actually runs at Stage 7: aggregate/population models (per-hop state = a `components: 1000` channel axis), representative explicit agents (a handful per generation), system-level analytics — the same 24×400-shaped ~10⁴-run campaign class as DSOC, inside every existing scale row. Nine scale worries judged **imaginary** for plausible workloads, with reasons on record: one-file-per-probe (unauthorable by grammar; `components` axis is the sanctioned shape); per-probe RunSpecs / 1M-agent execution; RunSpec JSON duplication (content-addressed store + derivable redundancy + transport zip); distributed execution now; graph/event databases for the corridor; streaming evidence "because decades" (mission time ≠ wall time; 7,305 samples at 1-day cadence is a small channel); hashed-record compression (container-independent channel hash bounds the future cost); topology/draw_order blow-up (scales with declared parameters); PostgreSQL/multi-writer. Two real, correctly-located concerns: hybrid-workload file counts (10⁷ worst case — ADR-011's dated `bench_package_scale` gate + the already-paid migration seam) and the `EpistemicSet` exhaustive-enumeration run-count wall (the §16 scenario matrix is a 324× outer multiplier; a declared fractional mode is an additive field when needed).

## 18. Optimization-interface recommendation

**The seam already exists; build nothing.** The evidence package *is* the machine-readable API (schema-validated JSON: metric results, sensitivity with mandatory convergence block, registers), doubled by ADR-024's uniform `--json`/exit-code contract. An external Pareto driver proposes draft designs (machine authoring at draft stage is explicitly permitted), iterates in the `exploratory` lane, and the chosen frontier points are re-frozen *attended* into evidence-grade campaigns — the two-lane design working as designed, not friction. Multi-objective needs no core change (six objectives are six `MetricSpec`s + criteria; nothing scalarizes; Pareto dominance over p-box envelopes is `analysis/` mathematics). Objective 6 ("minimize dependence on unvalidated assumptions") is computable *from the registers* — the registers are already the API for that axis. Seven anti-coupling tripwires to hold, named in the review: no optimizer imports (mechanized); no in-campaign adaptivity; no top-up promises; no utility/weight fields in hashed records; criteria frozen across a series (every threshold edit is a supersession + sign-off — an optimizer that tunes thresholds is automated tolerance-shopping); no unattended evidence-lane freezes; no result-push.

## 25. Final recommendation

**Adopt the text-only batch (four new ADRs, the seam amendments, the rejection list) into the current founder-review pass, and change nothing else.** The interstellar reference architecture is a strong *modelling* stress test and a weak *architectural* one: its §19 first question is the DSOC uncertainty-decomposition deliverable at larger scale; its epistemic-honesty and common-mode demands are ADR-004/010's existing core; its own §19–§20 discipline defers its optimization sections. The corpus was unusually self-aware — three of the five genuine gaps were already flagged in its own confidence tables — and this review's contribution is to convert those anticipations into decided seams while the price is still a sentence.
